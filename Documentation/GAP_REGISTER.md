## Headline for the CEO

The platform has **real, well-engineered depth in the middle** (payment orchestration, scheme adapters, settlement generation + reconciliation, commission-share config, the 8-step admin onboarding wizard, bitemporal config schema, ADR-016 anti-double-charge discipline) and is **hollow at both ends**: there is effectively **no authentication** in front of it, and **no production environment, monitoring, or backup** under it. Revenue booking on the newest corridor is miscategorised and lossy, and the partner-facing portal is largely fixtures.

**It is demoable. It is not sellable-with-a-signature yet.** The credible near-term sale is *operator-led onboarding + ZeroPay KRW pay-in*, demoed from the admin wizard, with everything else on a dated roadmap.

Blocker counts by lens: CPO 9 · CFO 3 · COO 5 · CISO 9 (overlaps consolidated below).

(2) **`refundAmountKrw` is persisted**: new field on `TransactionClient.StatusPatch` → `StatusPatchRequest` → `TransactionService.patchStatus`, applied (null-skipped) BEFORE the FSM transition, cumulative not per-refund, and left NULL for a non-KRW collection currency rather than mislabelling a foreign amount into a column settlement reads as KRW. `originalPaymentTxnRef` is also defaulted to the txnRef — **the second, unlisted reason the claw-back netted nothing**: `isCrossDateClawbackEligible` rejects any leg whose original-payment ref is blank, and it always was. (3) **`REFUNDED` now emits `payment.reversed`** (`PaymentReversedEvent.fromRefund`, `source=REFUND`, carrying the REFUNDED amount so a partial does not look full, and the refunded USD as `reversedUsd` so prefunding releases the right figure) — so revenue-ledger's reversal handler runs for a refund and prefunding gets a release signal. (4) **Refund webhook**: `notification-webhook` gains `PaymentReversedEventHandler` + `PaymentReversedKafkaConsumer` on `gmepay.payment.reversed`, registered in the existing `WebhookKafkaConsumerConfig` (same container factory, error handler and consumer group as approvals). **The signing model is untouched** — the existing dispatcher and `DefaultWebhookTargetResolver` sign a refund with that endpoint's own HKDF-derived, digest-verified secret exactly as they sign an approval (`RefundWebhookSigningTest` proves the signature verifies under that partner's secret and NOT under another's, and that an unprovable secret refuses delivery). (5) **Wallet refund** now patches `REFUNDED` (was `REVERSED`, which is why `findRefundedOn` never saw it — `refundedAt` is stamped only on entry to REFUNDED) and books a **real reversal journal for the refunded amount** instead of `postRoundingResidual(ref+"-REFUND", ZERO, "KRW")`; with no amount knowable it books nothing and says so, rather than a zero that reads as booked. Also: a LOCAL partner's refund is now booked at all (in the collection currency) — it previously fell through the OVERSEAS-only journal guard and was booked nowhere. **Scheme leg is deliberately FAIL-CLOSED**: the partial amount rides `SchemeClient.CancelRequest` and `RestSchemeClient` refuses it with 422 `PARTIAL_REFUND_UNSUPPORTED` **making no HTTP call**, because the ZeroPay adapter's cancel body is `{schemeTxnRef, reason}` and its 전문 cancel carries no amount — sending a full cancel for a partial refund would over-refund the customer at the scheme while our books recorded less.
